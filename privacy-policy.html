What the website does

Account Lens shows the Google identity information and optional profile details that Google returns after you authorize access. You can explore fictional sample information without signing in.

Information accessed

With standard Google sign-in, the website may receive a Google account identifier, name, email address and verification status, profile picture, preferred language, and token metadata. If you choose extra profile permissions, it may receive available birthday, address, phone number, gender, or organization details from the Google People API. Information depends on what Google actually returns.

How information is used and stored

Information is shown in your own authenticated session to demonstrate what has been shared. Google API access tokens are used for the requested fetch and then discarded, and refresh tokens are not requested. Your temporarily displayed account details are retained on the server in a session store (Redis when deployed), with sessions expiring after approximately one hour, or sooner if you sign out. The browser receives a session cookie with security settings, not Google OAuth access tokens.

Sharing and exports

This sample application does not intentionally send your returned Google profile data to an advertising service. The hosting service and Redis session provider process data to operate the website. If you export your information as JSON, the download is stored on your device; you are responsible for protecting that copy.

Your choices

You may decline optional scopes or sign out. Signing out deletes the website session; to remove Google's prior permission, visit your Google account connections. For a wider export of your Google data, use Google Takeout.

Security and changes

The deployed website should use HTTPS and a private session store, and administrators should protect environment secrets and hosting access. This document must be reviewed and updated by the website operator whenever the deployment or data-handling practices change.
